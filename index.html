Android application

Vacation Scheduler

Organize vacations and excursions in one place. Manage trip dates, hotel details, excursion schedules, reminders, and itinerary sharing.

Download Android APK

Requires Android 8.0 or later. This download is intended for Android devices.

Installation

  1. Download the APK on your Android device.
  2. Open the downloaded file.
  3. If Android prompts you, allow installations from the app you used to download the APK, then confirm installation.
  4. Open Vacation Scheduler from your app drawer.

This is a standalone Android application. Vacation and excursion data are stored locally on your device; an internet connection is not required for routine use.
